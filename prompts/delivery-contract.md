A `fleet` command that exits non-zero did **not** deliver — the pane may be dead or you may be sending too fast. Read stderr and act on it; do not assume a message arrived just because you sent it. A zero exit means the bytes reached a live terminal, which is still not a promise that the agent there has read them. Messages to `operator` end differently: the human has no terminal, so a zero exit prints **recorded** — it is in the fleet's log and their inbox, never in anyone's input box.
