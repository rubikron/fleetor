---
id: D-146
title: "an opencode seat's config dir is seeded from a snapshot of the operator's `opencode.json`; `OPENCODE_CONFIG` is dropped"
phase: "WP-32"
status: alive
---

- **What.** The seeder copies the operator's `~/.config/opencode/opencode.json` into the seat's `OPENCODE_CONFIG_DIR` (`seeds_from_operator: true`, as codex). Amends D-143, which pointed `OPENCODE_CONFIG` at the operator's file.
- **Why.** A relocated config dir drops the operator's global config and its providers (`opencode-spike-notes.md` §3). A snapshot restores them through the seeder the seam already has; `OPENCODE_CONFIG` would need a per-pane path the spec cannot carry as `'static` data.
- **Reverses if.** The operator wants edits to their own `opencode.json` to reach a live pane without a relaunch.
