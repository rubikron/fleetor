---
id: D-101
title: "`fleet task post --goal` is a flag; a task names its goal with `--parent`"
phase: S1
status: alive
---

- **What.** `post --goal` opens a goal. `post --parent 11` opens a task under goal 11. `post --goal 11` is refused with "to open a task under goal 11, use --parent 11" and is never parsed as opening a goal. Task numbers are accepted bare (`14`) or as a quoted `#14`; a subcommand that gets no number explains that the shell read `#` as a comment. `update` requires `--status`. The stored criteria field is `vision`; the flags stay `--crit-t` / `--crit-s`.
- **Why.** Matches the PRD's story 3 and its "optional goal (parent)" field, and adds no flag. The refusal covers the reading a model is most likely to try by analogy with `fleet handoff --goal 11`.
- **Reverses if.** Live runs show orch repeatedly typing `--goal <n>` despite the refusal.
