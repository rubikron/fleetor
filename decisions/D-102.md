---
id: D-102
title: "the hub takes the task store through `Hub::with_tasks`; `Hub::new` is unchanged"
phase: S1
status: alive
---

- **What.** `Hub::with_tasks(store, app, tasks, TaskContext { run, lineage })` attaches the target's task store. `Hub::new(store, app)` keeps its signature and refuses every `fleet task` with "no task store attached". The `fleet://task` pipe needs no new type: it is a second `BroadcastStore` over the task store.
- **Why.** `src-tauri` is owned by the launch PRD until its slice 1 merges, and it must keep compiling and passing against these crates unedited. Refusing is better than falling back to the run log, which would put tasks back where they rotate away.
- **Consequence.** Until the wiring half, the real app's `fleet task` refuses and the Tasks view is empty on this branch.
- **Reverses if.** After wiring nothing calls `Hub::new` outside tests; then the two constructors collapse into one.
