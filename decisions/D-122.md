---
id: D-122
title: "a receipt carries `tail`, the last lines of its check's output"
phase: meta
status: alive
---

- **What.** The `receipt` chain entry gains an optional `tail`: the check's merged stdout+stderr as `fleet done` captured it, ANSI stripped, last 20 lines, cut from the front at a line boundary to fit 4 KB. Omitted when the check printed nothing; old entries without it still parse. Amends D-107; the message `fleet done` sends and the receipt's other fields are unchanged.
- **Why.** The operator's call, 2026-10-15: the task page shows what the check said under the receipt.
- **Reverses if.** Tails bloat the store enough that the page should fetch output some other way.
