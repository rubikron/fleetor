---
id: D-128
title: "a cursor worker runs on the operator's own HOME and central login; it gets no private HOME"
phase: "WP-24"
status: alive
---

- **What.** A cursor worker seat answers checkpoint 6 with `private_home: false`: it runs under the operator's real `HOME`, so the login made once with `cursor-agent login` in a terminal is the one every cursor pane uses. Amends M8 and, for cursor workers only, D-052's private `HOME`.
- **Why.** Measured on `2026.10.22-27dcf5f`, zero tokens: cursor stores its login only in the macOS keychain. A private `HOME` reports `Not logged in`, a copied `~/.cursor` (M9) does not carry it, and `cursor-agent login` under a private `HOME` fails (`couldn't save your login to the macOS keychain`, exit 154) because that `HOME` has no default keychain. The operator's call, 2026-11-02: use the central login. Rejected: a per-seat keychain in each private `HOME` (unmeasured, a browser login per seat); `--api-key` (M8).
- **Cost.** A cursor worker can read what the Fence hides from other workers — `~/.ssh`, shell profiles, the login keychain. Containment of writes rests on cursor's sandbox (M11, M12), to be re-measured on this build.
- **Reverses if.** Cursor gains a file credential or a config-dir variable for its login, or a per-seat keychain is measured to work without touching the operator's search list.
- **Also.** The pane's configuration is still its own: `CURSOR_CONFIG_DIR` and `CURSOR_DATA_DIR` relocate it while the real `HOME` keeps the keychain login (`cursor-spike-notes.md` §2), so a pane's writes to `cli-config.json` never reach the operator's (M9's concern).
