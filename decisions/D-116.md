---
id: D-116
title: "every codex pane is seeded with `model_context_window = 872000`, a constant"
phase: meta
status: alive
---

- **What.** The codex seeder writes `model_context_window = 872000` into every pane's `config.toml`, orchestrator and workers, whether or not the seat names a model. An operator's own `model_context_window` is kept and nothing is announced. It is written in `install`, not as a spec key row.
- **Why.** Codex panes ran at each model's default 272000. 872000 is the catalog's `max_context_window` for every listed model as of 2026-10-10; it is deliberately assumed, not looked up per model, on the operator's instruction ("just assume 872k maximum"). Measured on codex-cli 0.161.0 with `gpt-5.6-luna`: the rollout's `token_count` reported 258400 without the key and 828400 with it (95% of each). Unmeasured: behaviour above a model's max (the hidden `gpt-5.5` has max 272000), and whether the plan accepts more than 272k of real input.
- **Reverses if.** A listed model's `max_context_window` differs from 872000, or a pane is refused or fails because of the window.
