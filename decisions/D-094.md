id: D-094
title: "dev mode and the live-run snapshot helpers are removed"
phase: "meta"
status: alive
---

- **What.** Dev mode is removed: the Settings switch, the coral band, `useDevMode`, the `dev_mode_get`/`dev_mode_set` commands, `src-tauri/src/dev.rs`, `Layout::dev_enabled` and `tests/dev_mode.rs`. So are `runs::snapshot_live_run` and `runs::live_run_id` with their tests. A `dev_mode` key left in `~/.fleetor/config.json` is ignored: the only reader takes `target` out of the JSON and nothing else, and a target write keeps other keys as they are.
- **Why.** D-092 removed their only consumers; a switch that changes nothing is a lie in the Settings panel.
- **Reverses if.** A grader or another dev-only posture returns.
