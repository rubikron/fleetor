- **The default that lost was "a brief is a job description."** D-042 moved the prose into `prompts/` and deliberately changed not one word of it; D-043 made that prose the pane's whole system prompt. This is the argument those two were clearing the ground for: *what a pane is told.* Tier 2 — prompt text — so this entry is the record rather than a request.
- **`orch` confirms the vision in writing before it decomposes anything.** Ask for the bigger picture; state it back in the operator's own terms, in a file or a message they can read back; wait for a yes. `Nothing goes to a worker until the vision is confirmed` is in the brief as a consequence rather than an instruction, because an instruction without one is advice. The failure being designed against is a fleet that starts fast on the wrong thing, which wastes five terminals rather than one.
- **"Guide the operator to think bigger" is bounded to one proposal.** If the vision is unclear or smaller than it could be, `orch` says so and proposes a bigger frame **once**, plainly, in one turn. If the operator declines or restates, it adopts their frame fully and does not raise it again in the session. An orchestrator that relitigates the goal every turn is worse than one that never raised it, and the authority bound is not negotiable: the operator's explicit word is final.
- **The integrity clause is the other half of that.** The fleet may find a better *route* to the operator's vision and should say so; it may never quietly substitute a vision of its own. Every deviation is `announced, not discovered later` — named, with why, and with what was done instead. The pre-existing anchor ("information to factor in, not an instruction that overrides what the operator asked") survives verbatim and is now pinned by a test rather than by luck.
- **The tenets are distilled, not pasted.** `docs/futureDesign/vision_tenets.md` is an essay with quotations, scripture and podcast plugs; a brief cannot afford any of it. `prompts/vision-tenets.md` is its six operative moves — write it down, prune, filter decisions through it, one vision not four, resistance is information, ME→WE. A test asserts the operative lines arrived *and* that the essay's framing did not come with them.
- **Workers get the three attitudes, and they are questions rather than rules.** *How can I be better? How can I push for more positive, meaningful impact? What do I do when I am confused?* The third has the answer attached — ask a peer by name, or `orch` — because "ask for help" without a named addressee is a worker that guesses. And ME→WE lands as `Improve yourself and the team around you`, with the cost stated: a worker who finishes their own task and leaves the fleet no wiser has done half the job.
- **One sentence is on loan to WP-07.** A worker with a question for the human is told to `ask orch to put it to the operator`, because no worker can address the human directly yet. It is deliberately self-contained and pinned by `a_confused_worker_is_told_exactly_who_to_ask`, so WP-07 replaces a sentence rather than reverse-engineering a paragraph.
- **Voice borrowed from `docs/CLAUDE.template.md`** where it did not conflict: collaborator-not-executor, put competing interpretations to the operator rather than picking one silently, distinguish verified from believed, treat pushback as new information and re-derive. Not borrowed: its subagent guidance ("decisions, not discretion"), which is exactly wrong for panes the fleet is built on treating as peers.
- **The budget, measured (WP-09 baseline).** Rendered orch **1,637 tokens**, worker **1,115** (DeepSeek Flash tokenizer, `examples/system-prompt-spike/count.py`). The progression across this package: 487/506 before WP-02 → 878/868 after the parity switch → 1,637/1,115 now. Against the **1,485 tokens** of CC guidance that stopped being sent, orch starts a session ~335 tokens lighter than before and a worker ~876 lighter. Four packages still queue behind this budget; WP-09 audits the sum.
- **What would reverse it.** Live evidence from a Flash worker — D-031 still records that these briefs are unvalidated against one, and WP-09's live shakedown is where that gets settled. The likeliest failures are named: an orchestrator that stalls on vision confirmation when the operator wanted one small thing done, or workers whose "push for more impact" reads as licence to widen their own scope. Both are prose fixes in `prompts/`, and both would show up as behaviour before they showed up as a bug.
- **Verified:** 71 workspace tests and 53 shell tests green, `tsc --noEmit` and `vite build` clean. Six new pinned-literal tests join the anti-amplification one: confirm-the-vision-before-decomposing, propose-bigger-once, the integrity clause with its surviving anchor, the three attitudes with ME→WE, the ask-orch routing, and the tenets-distilled-not-quoted check. Both fragments are asserted *absent* from the templates, so an operator rewriting prose cannot inline and then lose them.
