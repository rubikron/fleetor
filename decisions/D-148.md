- **What.** Slice 1 registers opencode with `Resume::NotSupported`, `read_usage` returning `None` (the rail says unavailable) and no transcript harvested. Slice 2 adds a pointer: the plugin records the pane's session ids in the seat dir, and resume, gauge and archive read the operator's `opencode.db` read-only through it.
- **Why.** The operator's call, 2026-11-02: vertical slices. A seat's sessions live in the operator's database (D-143), and a per-seat `OPENCODE_DB` logs the pane out (`No authenticated integrations`, measured), so the seat dir holds nothing those three checkpoints can read until the pointer exists. All three refusals are legal answers in the seam.
- **Reverses if.** Slice 2 lands, or opencode gains a per-seat session store that keeps the login.
