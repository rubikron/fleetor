---
id: D-130
title: "a cursor worker's brief is an alwaysApply rule in its worktree, appended under cursor's own prompt; cursor takes worker seats only"
phase: "WP-24"
status: alive
---

- **What.** A cursor worker's brief is written to `<worktree>/.cursor/rules/fleetor.mdc` at placement (`BriefCarrier::worktree_file`), beside the guardrail's `.claude/settings.local.json` (D-129). Both get a `/<path>` line in the repository's `info/exclude`. Cursor's brief is added after cursor's own system prompt, so D-043 (the brief replaces the vendor's prompt) does not hold for cursor workers.
- **Why.** `cursor-agent` has no flag that sets or replaces its system prompt (`--help`, build 2026.10.22-27dcf5f). The rule file is the only carrier measured to reach the model, and it survives `/clear` (`docs/notes/cursor-spike-notes.md`).
- **Workers only.** A harness that writes into its checkout cannot take the orchestrator seat, because that checkout is the operator's own repository, and the `.claude/settings.local.json` hook would then bind the operator's own Claude Code sessions there. `HarnessSpec::takes_the_operators_seat` derives this from the two worktree flags; `place_orch` refuses such a harness, a worker of one refuses the shared-checkout fallback, and the gate disables it on the orchestrator row.
- **Also.** The trust gate is seeded rather than flagged: `projects/<key>/.workspace-trusted` is written before launch and no `--trust` is passed. `CURSOR_DATA_DIR` is pointed at the pane's config dir, so trust and chats live under `~/.fleetor`.
- **Reverses if.** A cursor release adds a system-prompt flag (then the carrier becomes `argv_flag` and D-043 holds again), or the orchestrator seat is wanted on cursor (then its rule and hook files need a home outside the operator's repository).
