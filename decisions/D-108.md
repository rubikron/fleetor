id: D-108
title: "S3: the reviewer has its own rule, a verdict and a goal handoff are chain entries, and monitor flags are read off the chain in the UI"
phase: S3
status: alive
---

- **Reviewer.** A task field named with `fleet task post --reviewer 3` or `fleet task edit 14 --reviewer 3`. It has its own permission, apart from the edit rule: orch and the operator, on any task, including the operator's. The task's current owner cannot be named reviewer, and the named reviewer is refused when it takes the task up. A change writes a `reviewer-set` entry with the old and new pane. D-049 is amended: the reviewer is shown and never acted on.
- **Verdict.** `fleet task review 14 --met [--note …]` or `--not-met "<reason>"` writes a `reviewed` entry. Anyone but the owner; a goal is refused; `--not-met` needs its reason. The entry says whether its author was the named reviewer. It moves neither status nor owner.
- **Goal handoff.** `fleet handoff --goal 11` stays optional. With it the goal is closed and its chain gets a `handoff` entry listing the goal's tasks still planned or in progress; the run-log handoff event is written as before. A handoff naming a goal that cannot be closed records nothing.
- **Monitor flags.** Worked out in `ui/src/fleet/flags.ts` from the chain and the clock; nothing is stored and nothing acts on them. "Assigned, never taken up" after `NEVER_TAKEN_UP_MS` (10 minutes), shown as the elapsed time on the row, coral at the threshold. "Done, no receipt" looks for a receipt since the latest take-up. "Done, no verdict" looks for the named reviewer's verdict since the latest done. "Closed with N open tasks" is a closed goal with open tasks.
- **Worker cards.** The cards beside the terminals read the task store: the task a pane holds in this lineage, how long since it took it up, that task's latest entry, and the flags on the tasks it owns. A skipped review therefore flags the owner's card, not the reviewer's.
- **Evidence.** The task page lists every receipt under the technical criteria. It does not match a receipt to one criterion.
- **Measured, in rendered characters.** orch 18,561 with the longest startup text, worker 9,573; both under D-106's 18,900 and 9,750.
- **Not built here.** The owner and open/closed list filters (story 74), and an operator control for writing a verdict.
- **Reverses if.** Operators read a missing-verdict flag on the owner's card as the owner's fault; it then moves to the reviewer's card.
