---
id: D-112
title: "the write guardrail does not treat a standard stream device as a write target"
phase: meta
status: alive
---

- **What.** `offenders` in `write_guardrail.py` skips a candidate spelled `/dev/null`, `/dev/stdout`, `/dev/stderr`, `/dev/tty` or `/dev/fd/<digits>`, matched on the normalized absolute path and not the realpath. It applies to every candidate (`2>/dev/null`, `tee /dev/null`, `dd of=/dev/null`). Everything else under `/dev` is still refused, and D-065's scope is otherwise unchanged.
- **Why.** `cmd 2>/dev/null` was refused as a write outside the roots: four refusals across three panes in one live run, each pane rediscovering the workaround. Discarding output is not a write anyone guards. Per D-065 the fix is a rule and cases in `examples/write-guardrail-spike/cases.py`, not a wider root.
- **Reverses if.** A pane does damage through one of these five spellings, or the set needs to grow; then revisit the set rather than adding `/dev` to the roots.
