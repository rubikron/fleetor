---
id: D-098
title: "the wake waits for quiet first, and presses only a pane that will not settle by itself"
phase: meta
status: alive
---

- **What.** `wake` samples for silence before it presses anything. A pane that goes quiet on its own is announced with no keypress; only one still painting after `PRESS_AFTER` (3) consecutive noisy samples — about 1.2 s — gets the Enter it used to get on every pass. Amends #42's wake, which pressed first and then looked.
- **Why.** Measured through the registry on `codex-cli 0.160.0`: the old wake's Enter landed in a composer that was already reading, so every codex pane came up with the placeholder gone, an empty line in the composer, the cursor below the prompt and "tab to queue message" in the footer — the screen the operator reported. Brought up with no press at all, the same pane showed a clean composer. 0.153.4's splash, which the press exists for, animates indefinitely, so it still crosses the threshold and is still pressed.
- **Verified.** `a_woken_codex_pane_is_left_on_an_empty_composer` fails against the original loop and passes against this one. The bring-up and typing suites pass on both 0.160.0 and 0.153.4, including `a_message_delivered_the_instant_a_codex_pane_is_announced_is_submitted`.
- **Not verified.** That the footer meant a turn had actually started on every startup — it is what that hint usually means, and nothing here measured spend.
- **Reverses if.** A codex build's startup paints for longer than three samples without being on a splash, which would bring the stray Enter back; the threshold is the number to revisit.
