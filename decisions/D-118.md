id: D-118
title: "the gate offers one credential: every worker seat runs the operator's login (slice 1 of removing the DeepSeek key path)"
phase: meta
status: alive
---

- **What.** Nothing above placement knows a second credential. `SeatChoice.credential`, `CredentialChoice`, `settle_credentials`, `CredentialFallback`, `GateState.has_fleet_key` / `worker_model_default`, `FleetConfig.worker_backend` and `PaneRecord.credential` are gone; `FleetSeats::spec_for` puts every worker `PaneSpec` on the operator's plan. A worker seat on a harness with no readable login refuses the start, naming that harness's login command. A payload, remembered selection or run record that still says `credential` is read and the field ignored. Slice 2 removes the mechanism under `placement/`, `launch.conf` and the conformance suite; slice 3 the docs and superseded records.
- **Why.** The operator's call, 2026-10-10: the key path was DeepSeek-only and is not needed.
- **Supersedes.** The gate half of C75, C77 and C78, and all of C79, in `decisions.md`.
- **Reverses if.** A worker seat needs a credential other than the operator's own vendor login.
