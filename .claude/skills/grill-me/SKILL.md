Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decicions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask questions one at a time.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.

Make sure you think through possible branches and options before asking. Feel free to add branches and extend lines of thinking for 100% clarity (you may stop when user instructs you to stop).

At the end of a questioning session, print a small in terminal branch/tree diagram of the design tree that we have explored. This should include decisions made
