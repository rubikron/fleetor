title: "an opencode seat's posture is config (`permission`), read back by `debug agents`; `--auto` is dropped"
phase: "WP-32"
status: alive
---

- **What.** Every opencode pane runs `opencode --standalone` with `permission: {"*": "allow", "external_directory": "deny"}` in its seed and its `OPENCODE_CONFIG_CONTENT`. The gate reads the posture back from `opencode debug agents --standalone` under the same config: `approval` is the last `*`/`*` rule's effect, `filesystem` the last `external_directory` rule's, and the schema is pinned as `agents[].permissions[action,resource,effect]`. Amends D-144's mechanism, not its behaviour.
- **Why.** Checkpoint 3 needs a posture the spec names, and `--auto` takes no value. Config keys can be read back, as codex's are, so a retired key stops the fleet at the gate instead of spawning a pane that parks or widens. Measured on `opencode v2.2.5`: the `fleetor` agent resolves to `* → allow` and `external_directory → deny`.
- **Not measured.** That a real turn runs unprompted without `--auto`. Phase 4's live round trip is that measurement.
- **Reverses if.** A pane without `--auto` asks for approval on a call the permission rules allow.
