- **6,878 lines out.** Two crates (`fleetor-shim`, `fleetor-cc`), seven core modules, four server modules, nine test files, the fake-claude harness, every fixture.
- **`Op` is four variants and they took their plain names**, so the wire tag is the CLI verb exactly. `Assign` and `FleetStatus` were not kept "just in case" — they are the seed of the ticket system growing back.
- **`Hello` carries a `PaneId`, not an `Option<PaneId>` beside a `Party`.** That makes an anonymous connection unconstructible, so the test asserting one was refused had nothing left to check — which is a better outcome than a passing test.
- **`FleetEvent` is Message / PaneState / Notice.** A log able to describe a supervisor that no longer exists is a log that invites someone to make it exist again.
- **`Store` is `append_event` / `events_since` / `latest_seq`.** An append-only record of what the fleet said is the whole persistence story of a fleet whose members hold their own state in their own terminals — the same fact that made the mail queue unnecessary.
- **`Hub::new` requires an app.** A hub with nowhere to deliver could only ever refuse; the `Option` bought nothing but a branch.
- **Migration 0003 is appended, never edited**, and `DELETE FROM events` is deliberate: every row already in that log is a variant `FleetEvent` no longer has. Paired with it, `events_since` skips and warns on an undecodable payload rather than `?`-ing out — that read is the UI's entire history and runs on every boot, so one bad row used to mean a blank shell with no explanation (L9). A test asserts `events` is the only table left, so a stray `INSERT INTO tickets` cannot quietly compile somewhere.
- **Not done, out of scope until Phase 6:** README, `building.md` and `docs/fleet-comms-map.md` still describe the headless topology.
- **Verified:** `cargo test` green in both workspaces, `tsc --noEmit` and `vite build` clean, `fleet` builds. **Not verified:** the app launching as a window, and the manual demo behind it — both need a GUI session.
