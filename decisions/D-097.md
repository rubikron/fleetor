---
id: D-097
title: "a pane's colour query is answered at the pty, by the registry, and xterm is told not to answer"
phase: meta
status: alive
---

- **What.** The pump answers `OSC 10 ; ?` and `OSC 11 ; ?` itself, from the theme colours the interface sends through `pty_colors` at launch and on every theme change. `TerminalPane.tsx` registers OSC 10/11 handlers that claim the query, so xterm sends no second answer.
- **Why.** Codex draws its composer bar by blending from the terminal's background, which it asks for once, 0.22 s after it starts, and waits under 100 ms for (measured on `codex-cli 0.155.1`: answered at 80 ms it paints four background runs, at 100 ms none). xterm's own answer was a round trip through the event bus away and was refused on arrival besides, because the pane is still waking and `write` only reaches an announced pane. So a Fleetor pane never got the bar.
- **Verified.** `a_colour_query_is_answered_by_the_registry` (no vendor) and `codex_draws_its_composer_background_only_when_the_registry_answers` (real codex, with a control arm that paints none), three clean runs on `codex-cli 0.160.0`.
- **An unexplained hang, on the older build only.** On `codex-cli 0.155.1`, the same bring-up with colours answered repainted its composer forever in four consecutive runs — a field of fading braille dots — and the wake, which waits for silence, never returned. Codex was upgraded to 0.160.0 on this machine mid-session and it has not recurred in thirteen bring-ups since. Not understood; the wake has no deadline by design (D-034), so a recurrence shows as a pane that stays `spawning`.
- **Reverses if.** That hang recurs on a build in use. The switch is the `setTerminalColors` effect in `App.tsx`: without colours the registry answers nothing and panes come up as they did before.
