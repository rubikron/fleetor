- **What.** The list, the task page and the new task form follow the "Mockups" section of `docs/notes/fleetor-working-loop.html`; a recorded decision wins where the mockup predates it. Tabs `Open N` (planned, in-progress), `Closed N` (done, dropped) and `All N` (all but removed) replace the status filter, plus `Removed N` shown only when N > 0. They count tasks, not goals, and ignore the owner and run filters. Open is the default. A goal heads its group while any of its tasks is shown; a goal with no tasks stands on its own status. `this run` keeps a task with at least one chain entry from the live store's run; with no live run it is not offered. The task page and the new task form each replace the list, with a breadcrumb or Cancel back. Receipts show their output tail when the entry has one.
- **Why.** The operator's call, 2026-10-15.
- **Amends.** D-104's and D-113's status filter.
- **Reverses if.** The operator needs the list and a task's chain side by side; the page then returns as a panel.
