id: D-109
title: "a reopen follows the startup setting: its owners are told to continue, or the operator is asked first"
phase: S2
status: alive
---

- **What.** "Finish remaining tasks upon startup" applies to a reopened run as well as a new fleet. `{startup_tasks}` now has four renderings, chosen at launch from (new fleet or reopen) × (setting off or on):
  - new fleet, off: orch asks the operator about each in-progress task an earlier run left.
  - new fleet, on: orch resumes them and announces which.
  - reopen, off: orch asks the operator which in-progress tasks should continue, and tells no worker to continue before the answer.
  - reopen, on: orch tells each owner to continue the task it holds and announces which. It reassigns nothing an owner still holds.
  In every case planned tasks stay parked, and orch acts on its first message, not at launch (D-110).
- **Why.** Decided by the operator on 2026-10-07 after a live reopen where every pane sat idle. A reopen brings back the same conversations, so ownership survives it, but nothing told the owners to pick their tasks back up. The setting is named for startup, and a reopen is a startup.
- **Amends.** D-106 and the task PRD's coordination rule D, which said the triage and `{startup_tasks}` apply to fresh launches only. Still true from rule D: a reopen keeps its lineage, owners keep their tasks, and the worker brief's reopen rule sends a worker to `fleet task show` before it continues.
- **Reverses if.** Reopened workers, told to continue, redo work a later session already finished despite the reopen rule.
